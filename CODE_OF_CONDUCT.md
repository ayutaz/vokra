# Code of Conduct

**English** | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ja.md)

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Discuss ideas and observable
behavior rather than a person's identity or assumed motives. Make room for
different experience levels, languages, and accessibility needs, and accept
good-faith correction.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or sustained
  personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, intimidation, spam, or repeated off-topic contact.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and other
spaces where someone is acting on behalf of Vokra. It governs participation,
not disagreement: technical criticism and declining a contribution are not
violations when expressed respectfully.

## Moderation and reporting

The project does not publish a private conduct-reporting address. For behavior
visible on GitHub, use the reporting, blocking, and moderation controls in the
GitHub interface when appropriate. Do not publish private personal details in
an issue or pull request.

The maintainer may moderate visible repository activity. A report about the
sole maintainer has no independent project-level review path; GitHub's
platform-level reporting process remains available. Security vulnerabilities
must instead use the private channel in the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Responses may include a clarification, warning, removal or locking of content,
temporary participation limits, or a permanent ban. The response should be
proportionate to impact, pattern, and risk of recurrence. Decisions are
documented publicly when doing so does not expose private or sensitive
information.
